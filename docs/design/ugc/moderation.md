# Moderation

_Resolution: question_

Content arrives from people you do not know. Nothing about how that is handled
is decided.

## What is known

There is [no server](../multiplayer/multiplayer.md), so there is no place to
review content before it reaches a player and no authority to remove it after.
Whatever moderation means here, it is not a queue someone approves.

Technical safety is already covered: [the format](format.md) is data only and
validated before opening, so hostile content cannot execute. This section is
about content that is legal to load and still unwelcome.

Whether player-made items participate in [rarity](../loot/rarity.md) depends on
what can be trusted, so that question waits on this one.

## Open

Whether trust is per-player, per-group, or absent by design.

Whether a place carries provenance a player can see before entering.

What a player can do about content they do not want, given nobody can remove it
for them.

## What would settle it

A decision on how content moves between players. If it only ever arrives from
someone you chose to play with, most of this question disappears.
