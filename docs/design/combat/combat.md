# Combat

_Resolution: question_

You fight things. Almost nothing else about combat is decided, and this section
exists to hold the shape of the question rather than to answer it.

## What is known

Combat happens in the world at the same scale the player moves at, so it is
constrained by [movement](../movement/movement.md) rather than switching to a
separate mode.

An enemy's intent is readable before it lands. Whatever the mechanics turn out
to be, a player should be able to see that something is about to happen and
where, which is the [legibility pillar](../pillars.md) applied here.

Enemies may occupy more than one cell, and size means something: reach, threat,
footprint. A large enemy is one composition rather than several adjacent things.

Combat drops things, so it touches [loot](../loot/loot.md).

## Why this is not being probed yet

Movement and [world structure](../world/structure.md) both change what combat
can be. Deciding combat first would mean deciding it twice.

## Open

Whether combat is real time, paused, or something between.

Whether attacks occupy cells, arcs, or free-form areas.

Whether there is a dash, and whether it belongs here or to movement.

## What would settle it

Movement being built and felt, plus a decision on world structure. Then one
enemy and one attack, in the world, at real speed.
